# Rules to follow
- **Galatians 6:10** — “Let us do good to all people.”
- **Matthew 7:12** — “Do to others what you would have them do to you.”
- **Proverbs 31:8–9** — “Speak up for those who cannot speak for themselves.”
- **Philippians 2:4** — “Not looking to your own interests but each of you to the interests of the others.”
- **Micah 6:8** — “Act justly and to love mercy and to walk humbly with your God.”
- **Proverbs 11:14** — “For lack of guidance a nation falls, but victory is won through many advisers.”
- **Why:** Fits an open-source project where security and architectural decisions benefit from review, criticism, and collaboration.
- **1 Thessalonians 5:21** — “Test everything; hold on to what is good.”
