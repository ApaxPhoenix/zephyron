The first messages over Tor: the courier carries them from one phone's onion service to another's.

## Features

- The courier, which sends messages between trustees over their onion services
- The conversation page, with attachments
- The relays page, showing the Tor circuits your traffic takes
- A new registration screen and a redesigned network page
- Identity stores bound to the phone's secure hardware
- A disappearing message timer
- A duress passphrase that opens a decoy identity and quietly erases the rest
- A theme around Geist

## Known issues

- Messages are protected by Tor's encryption alone; end-to-end encryption comes next
- A message to someone who is offline waits on your phone until they are back
- Tor runs only on Android
