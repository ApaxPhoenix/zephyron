Communities, groups and calls, and messages that wait for people who are offline.

## Features

- Peer-to-peer communities with no server and no owner, with text, podcast and live voice channels
- Removal by vote: once more than half agree, the community key changes so the removed person reads nothing new
- Proof of work on joining and rate limits per member, to keep bots out
- Voice messages up to ten minutes, video messages with a thumbnail, photos, files up to 25 MB and GIFs
- Podcast episodes up to an hour, shared between members, and voice rooms for six
- Private groups that sit next to your direct chats
- Voice calls with a trustee, and group calls
- Messages for someone offline held sealed by a trustee, or by a mailbox phone you choose, until they are back
- Post-quantum sessions that open with X3DH plus ML-KEM-768 on Android
- An opt-in Discover directory where people and communities can choose to be listed, shared between phones with no server
- Traffic padded to fixed sizes, so message lengths do not show

## Fixes

- Unlocks are watched across every identity together, so rotating identities or one passphrase tried on many is caught

## Known issues

- Tor runs only on Android; the other platforms build without it
- Post-quantum sessions fall back to classical encryption on iOS
- Some pairs of phones never reach each other, though both are online
