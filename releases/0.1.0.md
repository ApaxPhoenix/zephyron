The first working Zephyron: private chats between trustees, phone to phone over Tor, with no server in between.

## Features

- Identities that live only on the phone: a private key, a recovery phrase and a passphrase, and several identities on one device
- An onion service per identity, run by a Tor bundled with the app, with obfs4 bridges where Tor is blocked
- Chats end to end encrypted with the Double Ratchet, stored in a SQLCipher database whose key comes from the passphrase and the phone's secure hardware
- Trustees: requests from strangers stay apart until you accept them, and blocked addresses are dropped
- Disappearing messages, from five minutes to a week, per chat
- An offline map with location search, downloaded once
- A vault that locks itself after ten minutes, rate-limited unlocks that erase an identity after ten wrong passphrases, a duress passphrase and a panic wipe
- Screenshots and screen recording blocked, a photo cropper for avatars and banners, and freeRASP's checks for root, hooks and tampering in release builds

## Fixes

- Frames are encrypted end to end, not only by Tor

## Known issues

- Tor runs only on Android; the other platforms build without it
- A message to someone who is offline still waits on your phone until they are back
- Communities are an early preview: their model and mesh are in, their screens are not
