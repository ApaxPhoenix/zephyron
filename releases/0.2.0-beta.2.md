Messages that wait for people who are offline: a trustee or a mailbox phone holds them sealed until they are back.

## Features

- A trustee, or a mailbox phone you choose, holds sealed messages for people who are offline and passes them on when they are back
- A mailbox on your profile, and delivery settings for what this phone carries for others

## Known issues

- There are no calls yet
- Private groups and the Discover directory are still to come
- Post-quantum sessions fall back to classical encryption on iOS
