Communities: peer-to-peer groups with text, podcast and live voice channels, voice messages in chats, and post-quantum sessions.

## Features

- Communities with no server and no owner, with their own pages and a communities tab
- Voice messages and video messages in chats
- Podcast episodes shared between members, and live voice rooms
- Removal by vote: once more than half agree, the community key changes
- Proof of work on joining and rate limits per member, to keep bots out
- Post-quantum sessions that open with X3DH plus ML-KEM-768 on Android, through the bundled liboqs
- Traffic padded to fixed sizes and sent after a short random delay
- Thumbnails for video messages

## Known issues

- A message to someone who is offline still waits on your phone until they are back
- There are no calls yet
- Post-quantum sessions fall back to classical encryption on iOS
